Acme Corporation Employee Handbook — Selected Policies

Remote work is permitted up to three days per week for all full-time employees, subject to manager approval. Employees accrue twenty days of paid vacation leave each year, accrued monthly and carried over up to a maximum of five days. Business expenses must be submitted within thirty days of purchase, with itemized receipts, in order to be reimbursed. Employees are required to complete annual security awareness training before the end of the first quarter.

Globex Logistics — Warehouse Standard Operating Procedures

Temperature-sensitive shipments must be kept within the cold chain between two and eight degrees Celsius at all times during storage and transit. Damaged goods must be photographed and reported to the warehouse supervisor within twenty-four hours of delivery, and quarantined pending inspection. Every outbound pallet must be scanned and assigned a tracking number before it leaves the loading dock. Hazardous materials must be stored separately from food-grade products in clearly labeled containment areas.
